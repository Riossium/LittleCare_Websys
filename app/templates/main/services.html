Our Capabilities

Comprehensive Baby Healthcare Services

Explore how LittleCare provides an all-in-one healthcare management experience for families and doctors.

Centralized Baby Health Records

Store and organize all health events, well-baby checkup notes, prescriptions, laboratory attachments, and milestone observations in one secure, accessible place. Filter easily by category and access historical records whenever visiting a new healthcare provider.

  • Multi-baby profile support under a single parent account
  • Secure document upload (prescriptions, lab tests, certificates)
  • Automatic age calculation in months and days

Pediatric Growth Monitoring

Track your baby's physical development with interactive Chart.js growth charts. Compare recorded weight, length/height, and head circumference against official World Health Organization (WHO) Child Growth Standards.

  • Dynamic visual percentile comparisons (Median, P15, P85)
  • Calculated Body Mass Index (BMI) tracking
  • Monthly growth update reminders

Immunization Schedule Management

Never lose track of a vaccine dose again. LittleCare tracks childhood vaccines from birth through toddlerhood (BCG, Hepatitis B, Pentavalent, Polio, PCV, Rotavirus, MMR) and triggers automatic notifications when upcoming doses are due.

  • Complete vaccine logs with batch numbers and administering clinic
  • Scheduled, completed, and missed status tracking
  • Automated 7-day advance reminders

AI Healthcare Assistant

Powered by OpenAI and pediatric knowledge engines, our AI assistant answers everyday parenting and baby healthcare inquiries. With parental authorization, it retrieves your baby's age and health records to provide personalized educational guidance.

  • Safe pediatric guardrails (no diagnosis, no prescription)
  • Red-flag emergency symptom detection and urgent advisory
  • Interactive suggested next steps & relevant library articles
