About LittleCare

Dedicated to Simplifying Infant Healthcare for Every Family

Caring for a newborn or infant is one of life's most rewarding experiences, but managing pediatric schedules, vaccination cards, growth milestones, and medical histories across different clinics can quickly become overwhelming.

LittleCare was built to give parents peace of mind. By combining intuitive health record management, evidence-based WHO growth tracking, verified doctor consultations, and a scientifically grounded AI assistant, LittleCare bridges the gap between everyday home care and professional pediatric guidance.
