Data Privacy & Security Policy

LittleCare operates in strict adherence to Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA) of the Philippines, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. As a healthcare information management platform, we prioritize the confidentiality and integrity of infant health records above all else.

1. Personal & Sensitive Health Data We Collect

To deliver personalized healthcare management services, we process information including:

  • Parent & Guardian Account Information: Full name, verified email address, contact numbers, hashed passwords.
  • Baby Health Profile Data: Baby’s name, date of birth, sex, birth weight, birth height, head circumference, blood type, known allergies, and medical checkup notes.
  • Immunization & Clinical Records: Administered vaccines, batch details, scheduled visits, and uploaded medical documentation.
  • Physician Professional Credentials: PRC Medical License numbers, clinic affiliations, practice specialties, and availability schedules.

2. Role-Based Access Control & Strict Parent Ownership

Baby health profiles are linked directly to authorized parent accounts. Other parents cannot view or search your baby's medical information. Doctors only receive temporary, read-only access to a baby's clinical summary when you explicitly request and book an appointment with that physician.

3. AI Context Retrieval & Parental Authorization

The LittleCare AI Healthcare Assistant requires explicit opt-in parental authorization before referencing baby profile data. When granted, only essential context (age, latest measurements, allergies) is provided to provide tailored educational guidance. All prompts and API credentials are kept securely on our backend servers and never exposed to public frontend scripts.

4. Audit Trails & Security Measures

All sensitive access events (viewing patient records, AI context retrieval, verification approvals) are captured in our tamper-resistant security audit log for accountability.
